## 📊 Next Steps for Better Rankings

### Immediate Actions (Do This Week):

1. **Submit to Google Search Console**
   - Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Add property: https://bashies.co.za
   - Submit sitemap: https://bashies.co.za/sitemap.xml
   - Monitor indexing status

2. **Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools**
   - Go to: https://www.bing.com/webmasters
   - Add site and submit sitemap

3. **Google Business Profile (CRITICAL!)**
   - Create/claim: https://business.google.com
   - Add accurate business info (address, hours, phone)
   - Upload photos of products and store
   - Get customer reviews (this is huge for local SEO!)

### Medium-Term (1-2 Months):

4. **Create Blog Content**
   - "How to Make Biltong at Home"
   - "Biltong vs Jerky: What's the Difference?"
   - "Best Game Meats for Biltong"
   - "The History of South African Biltong"

   Each blog post = more keywords, more pages to rank

5. **Get Backlinks**
   - Get listed on:
     - Cape Town tourism sites
     - Local business directories (Yelp, TripAdvisor)
     - Food blogs in Cape Town
     - South African expat communities
   - Partner with local businesses for cross-promotion

6. **Improve Page Speed**
   - Optimize images (compress product photos)
   - Use WebP format for images
   - Enable lazy loading
   - Use CDN for faster delivery

7. **Add FAQ Section**
   - Use FAQ schema markup
   - Answer common questions about biltong
   - Helps appear in Google's "People Also Ask" section

### Advanced (3+ Months):
